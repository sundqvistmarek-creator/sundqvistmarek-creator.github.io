Steps and Instructions For 9" Double-Crust Pie

Ingredients Measurements
Flour 2 cups
Salt 1 tsp
Ice Water 4 Tbs
Butter 14 Tbs
Sliced Apples 4 cups
Cinnamon or Nutmeg 1 tsp
Sugar 3/4 to 1 cup
  1. First gather all ingredients for the crust
    • 2 cups flour
    • 1 tsp salt
    • 2/3 cup + around 2 Tbs butter
    • 4 Tbs ice water
  2. Pre-heat oven to 350°F
  3. Measure out all ingredients into a bowl and slowly add water
  4. Make a second round of dough for a double crust
  5. After the dough is mixed, press out the crust into a 9" pie dish
  6. Bake for 10 min until light brown
    • Silicon crust protectors can be used to prevent burning especially on the second time in the oven
  7. Then gather all of your ingredients for the filling
    • 3/4 to 1 cup sugar
    • 1 tsp cinnamon or nutmeg
    • 6 to 7 cups sliced apples
    • 1 1/2 Tbs butter
  8. Peel skin off apples (optional)
  9. Mix in all ingrediants into a bowl and add a squeeze of lime juice
  10. Pour filling into pie and place the second layer of crust on top
  11. Finally, bake on 350 for 1 hour or until light golden

Tips and Tricks

  • Substitute lard for some of the butter to achieve an flaky crust
  • Rolling out the top crust with a roller makes it more even
  • Sometimes cooking the filling in a pot before putting it in the pie is a quicker option
